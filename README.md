# Smartypant Social Autopilot

Social media on autopilot: review, approve and edit the posts Smartypant creates for your business.

Smartypant is a social media autopilot for small businesses. It learns your business, brand and industry, then uses industry-specific content workflows to decide what to publish and create the content: image posts with captions, multi-shot Reels edited to music, and talking-avatar videos when useful. Smartypant then publishes the finished content on schedule to your connected social accounts.

Smartypant also learns from your preferences, approvals and edits over time, so future content becomes better aligned with your business. Content can be corrected or regenerated in Smartypant when something does not come out as intended.

This plugin lets you review and control that work without leaving the conversation:

- See your upcoming or recently published posts, including images and videos
- Approve posts or undo an approval
- Change a caption by telling Claude what you want

Co-pilot mode: posts are published after your approval.
Autopilot mode: Smartypant handles the recurring publishing automatically.

Requires a Smartypant account with at least one connected social media account. Business setup and creation of new content currently happen in Smartypant.

Example prompts: "Show my Smartypant posts for this week", "Approve my next post", "Make the caption of my next post shorter".

## Setup

1. Add the plugin, then connect Smartypant Social Autopilot on the plugin's Connectors tab.
2. Log in with your Smartypant account and click Allow.

You need a Smartypant account (https://smartypant.xyz) with at least one connected social media account. Business setup and the creation of new content happen in the Smartypant app.

## Data

The plugin contains one skill with usage instructions and a reference to the Smartypant connector at https://mcp.smartypant.xyz/mcp. Through that connector, Claude reads your planned and recent posts (date, platform, caption, links to images and videos, approval status) and, when you ask, approves posts, undoes approvals or replaces a caption. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends data nowhere else. Details: https://smartypant.xyz/privacy-policy
